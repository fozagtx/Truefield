# Lockout/Tagout procedure LOTO-PUMP-07 (SYNTHETIC plant procedure for the demo)

> Written to be consistent with OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 (The control of hazardous energy). This is a demo document, not a certified procedure.

## Step 1. Notify
"Notify the area operator and all affected employees that PUMP-07 will be shut down and locked out."

## Step 2. Shut down
"Stop PUMP-07 from the local station using the normal stop button."

## Step 3. Electrical isolation (energy source: electrical)
"Open the motor breaker at MCC-2 bucket 3B. Apply personal lock and tag. Verify zero energy by attempting a try-start at the local push-button station."

## Step 4. Mechanical (energy source: mechanical)
"Wait for zero shaft rotation. Fit the coupling block to prevent rotation of the shaft."

## Step 5. Lubrication circuit (energy source: stored)
"Isolate oil pump OP-07 at its local isolator, lock and tag. Relieve circuit pressure through bleed valve BV-07 until the gauge reads 0 bar."

## Step 6. Thermal
"If the housing is above 60 °C, allow it to cool. Verify below 45 °C with an IR thermometer before contact."

## Step 7. Removal of locks
"Only the person who applied a lock may remove it. Record the name of the person removing locks on the close-out form."
